## What will and won't carry over

Cost mechanics travel: subscription tiers, **payment processing fees**, the apps
you end up needing, maintenance hours. Those are the same objects in every
market, and they are what this site exists to compute.

French regulatory pages do **not** travel. They stay a local asset, and the
English edition will build its own where the rules differ.

## What this site is not

No get-rich methods. No revenue screenshots. No income promises — to you or to
anyone.

Ranking [methodology](/fr/methode/) is published and **no placement is for
sale**. This site is not a consultancy and gives no legal advice.
